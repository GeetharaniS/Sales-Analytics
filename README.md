# 📊 Telecom Sales Analytics & Customer Segmentation

An end-to-end **Telecom Sales Analytics and Customer Intelligence project** designed to analyze customer behaviour, monitor business performance, predict customer churn, and develop targeted marketing strategies using data analytics, business intelligence, and machine learning.

## 🚀 Project Overview

The project focuses on transforming telecom customer and transaction data into actionable business insights.

It combines descriptive, predictive, and customer segmentation analytics to help businesses:

- Monitor revenue and subscriber performance.
- Understand customer recharge and usage behaviour.
- Identify high-value and at-risk customers.
- Predict customer churn and future business trends.
- Develop personalized marketing and customer retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze telecom sales and recharge performance.
- Track customer acquisition, retention, and churn.
- Understand customer spending patterns.
- Segment customers based on their purchasing behaviour.
- Identify opportunities to increase customer lifetime value.
- Support data-driven business and marketing decisions.

## 📈 Project Modules

### 1. Telecom Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide insights into telecom business performance.

Key analytical areas:

- Total revenue and recharge performance.
- Active and inactive subscribers.
- Customer distribution and behaviour.
- Prepaid and postpaid analysis.
- Call and mobile data usage.
- Customer and revenue trends.

**Dashboard File:** `Telecom_Dashboard.pbix`

**Dashboard Report:** `Telecom_dashboard.pdf`

### 2. Predictive Analytics

The predictive analytics component aims to use historical customer and transaction data to forecast business outcomes and support proactive decision-making.

Planned analytical use cases include:

| Use Case | Analytical Approach |
|---|---|
| Customer Churn Prediction | Random Forest / Gradient Boosting |
| Next Recharge Amount | Regression |
| Subscriber Growth Forecasting | ARIMA / Prophet |
| Network Demand Forecasting | Time-Series Analysis |
| Next Best Offer | Recommendation Systems |

### 3. RFM Customer Segmentation

RFM analysis is used to categorize customers based on their transaction behaviour.

**RFM stands for:**

- **Recency:** How recently a customer recharged.
- **Frequency:** How frequently a customer recharged.
- **Monetary:** How much a customer spent.

The analysis helps identify different customer groups and design appropriate marketing strategies.

| Customer Segment | Marketing Strategy |
|---|---|
| Champions / VIP | Exclusive rewards and loyalty benefits |
| Loyal Customers | Retention offers and premium plans |
| Potential Loyalists | Encourage frequent recharges |
| New Customers | Welcome offers and onboarding |
| Promising Customers | Personalized promotions |
| Need Attention | Targeted recharge incentives |
| About to Sleep | Engagement campaigns |
| At Risk | Win-back offers |
| Can't Lose Them | Personalized retention campaigns |

**Analysis File:** `Telecom_RFM_Analysis.xlsx`

## ▶️ Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Python 3.x
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft Excel
- Jupyter Notebook (if working with notebooks)

### Steps

1. Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/GeetharaniS/Sales-Analytics.git
```

2. Navigate to the project directory:

```bash
cd Sales-Analytics
```

3. Extract `telecom_dataset.zip` if you want to work with the source data.

4. Open `Telecom_Dashboard.pbix` using Power BI Desktop.

5. Open `Telecom_RFM_Analysis.xlsx` using Microsoft Excel.

6. Explore the predictive analytics resources in the `Predictive analytics` folder.

7. Refer to the Business Requirements Document for project objectives and business context.
